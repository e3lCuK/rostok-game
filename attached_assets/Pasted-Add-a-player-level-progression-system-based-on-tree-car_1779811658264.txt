## PART 2 — LEVEL XP LOGIC

After every completed session:

Take all 3 activity percentages:

Water %
Light %
Fertilizer %

Calculate average:

carePercent =
(water + light + fertilizer) / 3

Example:

100 + 80 + 60 = 240
240 / 3 = 80

Result:
+80 XP

---

## IMPORTANT

XP gained per session equals average care percentage.

Maximum possible:
100 XP

(Perfect score should remain nearly impossible.)

---

## PART 3 — XP STORAGE

* totalXP

Refactor income history display: simplify on Home page and add detailed session history on Game page.

---

## GOAL

Split history logic into two layers:

1. **Home page → simplified (merged entries)**
2. **Game page → detailed per-session breakdown**

---

## PART 1 — HOME PAGE (SIMPLIFY)

### PROBLEM

Currently showing:

* "Активный (база)"
* "Активный (бонус)"

This creates duplicate entries for one session.

---

### REQUIRED

Merge base + bonus into ONE entry per session.

---

### IMPLEMENTATION

When rendering history:

Group entries by session (same sessionId or timestamp window)

Then:

const total = baseAmount + bonusAmount

Render:

"Активный вклад"
+{total} ₽

---

### RESULT

Instead of:

Активный (база)  +109 ₽
Активный (бонус) +17 ₽

Show:

Активный вклад +126 ₽

---

### IMPORTANT

* Do NOT change backend logic
* Only merge at UI level
* Keep sorting (latest first)

---
